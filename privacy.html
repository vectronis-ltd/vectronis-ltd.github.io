Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026. Applies to the website vectronis.co.uk.

Who we are

VECTRONIS LTD is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17533965). Registered address: First Floor Office, 3 Hornton Place, London W8 4LZ, United Kingdom. You can contact us at info@vectronis.co.uk.

What this website collects

This website is a static site. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not ask you to create an account or fill in forms.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in server logs for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When the page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.

If you email us

When you contact us by email, we keep your message and contact details for as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of our correspondence. We do not share them with third parties except where required by law. Our email is provided by Namecheap Private Email.

Our apps

Our products, including Finlens, have their own privacy policies that describe what each app does with your data. Those will be published alongside each app in the App Store and Google Play.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email info@vectronis.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
